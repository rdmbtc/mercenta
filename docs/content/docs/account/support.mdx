## Contact support

Email [support@mercenta.xyz](mailto:support@mercenta.xyz) for order or account help. Include your order reference, timestamp, public transaction hash if relevant, and the exact error. Do not send wallet keys, passwords or delivery codes. No response-time guarantee is implied.

## Official news channels

- [Mercenta official — English](https://t.me/mercenta)
- [Mercenta RU — русский](https://t.me/mercentacis)

These are announcement channels, not private order-support chats. Use the support email for account-specific issues.

Never share seed phrases, private keys, API secrets or one-time delivery codes. A support case may include your public transaction hash, account/order reference, timestamp and error code.
