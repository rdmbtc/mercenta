## Start with a plan

In [the workspace](https://app.mercenta.xyz/app?section=budget), open **Account → Budget**. Or choose **Plan my budget** on Home. Quick setup lets you choose a personal or business example and edit the allocation before continuing.

A preview is not a saved plan. Connect your wallet and sign in to save it. You do not need to send funds to explore the preview.

## Choose three limits

- **Monthly total:** your calendar-month plan, measured in UTC.
- **Per-order cap:** the most a single new Shop order may spend.
- **Daily cap:** the most new Shop orders may spend in a UTC day.

Allocate 100% across purchases/restock, reserve, profit goal and optional tax reserve. The per-order cap must not exceed the daily cap; the daily cap must fit within your monthly purchase allocation.

Personal and reseller percentages are editable examples, not recommendations or financial advice. Keeping a reserve in your plan does not move funds into a separate wallet.

## Save, then review

Select **Save budget** after signing in. A saved budget is checked by the backend when a new prepaid Shop purchase is confirmed, including purchases initiated through the assistant or API. It uses exact six-decimal USDC amounts.

Another tab may change your plan. A revision conflict asks you to refresh rather than silently overwriting it. Do not mistake a connection error for a successful save.

## Know the boundary

Shop limits do **not** cap external wallet transfers, Circle Gateway payments or Profit First contract allocations. Percentages do not reserve, lock, invest or transfer money. A profit goal is not verified profit, tax liability or an Earn deposit.

[Choose a product](/docs/account/orders) once you understand your limits.
