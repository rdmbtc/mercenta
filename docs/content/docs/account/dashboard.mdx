## Before you connect a wallet

[Home](https://app.mercenta.xyz) introduces the test catalogue. Select **View details** to inspect a product or **Browse products** to open Shop. Wallet sign-in is not required for browsing.

The sample products are simulated, not live AppRoute stock or redeemable digital codes. Preview prices are not purchase authorization.

## After you sign in

Home can show your available prepaid account balance, recent purchases and recorded spending. Private data is loaded for the authenticated wallet, never inferred from a public address or invented for a demonstration.

An unavailable figure is not zero. A **Last loaded account balance** is retained information, not a verified current balance. Refresh and reconcile before relying on it for a purchase.

## Find the next action

- **Shop → Purchases:** inspect your original order and its status.
- **Account → Balance:** top up the prepaid account with test USDC.
- **Account → Activity:** inspect recorded account movements.
- **Account → Budget:** set limits for new Shop purchases.
- **Assistant → Journal:** keep manual notes separate from the ledger.

Optional quick setup helps you preview an editable monthly plan. It does not transfer funds, save an order or activate live products.
