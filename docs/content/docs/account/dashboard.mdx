---
title: "Dashboard"
description: "Orders, channel spend and top services."
---

Choose Last 7 days, Last 30 days, All time or a custom UTC date interval. Custom dates include the entire end day.

- Total orders, split into Shop and Direct Top-Up.
- Total spent in the selected period, split by channel.
- Available and reserved account balance.
- Top 10 services by spend and their order count.
- Recent activity with initiator, reference, product and status.

An Initiator is `Portal` or `API key:<key-id>`. Metrics come from this account's recorded test orders. Zero means no records; unavailable data is not replaced with fabricated activity. CSV export respects the selected filters.
