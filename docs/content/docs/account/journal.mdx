## Open your journal

Choose **Assistant → Journal** in [Mercenta](https://app.mercenta.xyz/app?section=journal). Sign in with your wallet to load and save private entries.

Record a manual expense, declared income or note. These records do not change your prepaid balance, prove a sale or create an onchain transaction. Avoid counting a Shop purchase twice by recording it again as additional spending.

## Keep private details private

Journal text is wallet-scoped and encrypted at rest on the server. This is **not end-to-end encryption**. Do not store wallet keys, passwords, API credentials or redeemable codes in entries.

You can export your entries to CSV. Keep exported files private.

## Ask for educational guidance

The financial coach has no tools or transaction authority. An explicit consent checkbox allows your question and numeric aggregates to be sent to the configured AI provider. Note text and wallet address are not included in that aggregate payload; your own question can still disclose personal information.

Output is untrusted educational text, not financial, tax or legal advice. A labelled rules-based fallback may appear when the model is unavailable. Neither path can purchase or stake for you.

[Assistant purchase proposals](/docs/agents/ai-advisor) are a separate flow and require explicit confirmation.
