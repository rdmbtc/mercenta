---
title: "Earn sandbox"
description: "Understand yield without moving account funds."
---

The treasury lab uses 5.4% APY as a labelled calculation assumption, not current or guaranteed live yield. Sandbox deposit and withdrawal affect only a separate per-session treasury model.

Customer account deposits do not earn interest automatically. Earn discovery may query the installed SDK, but unavailable vault discovery returns unavailable data rather than fabricated APY. Before live execution, verify vault addresses, network support, user signatures, receipts and risk disclosures.
