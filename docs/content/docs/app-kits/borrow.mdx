---
title: "Borrow sandbox"
description: "cirBTC risk modelling, not live credit."
---

The lab assumes a cirBTC price of 92,400 USDC, 75% maximum LTV and an 82.5% liquidation threshold. These are labelled scenario inputs, not a live oracle.

The model calculates maximum borrowing, health factor and liquidation price with integer arithmetic. It does not deposit real collateral, create debt on-chain or credit the customer account.

Live borrowing requires verified market availability, fresh oracle evidence, human signing and independently checked settlement. Do not sell a simulator as a credit product.
