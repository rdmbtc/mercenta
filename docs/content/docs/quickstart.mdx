## 1. Open the account

Open the deployed Mercenta console or `http://localhost:3011/app` locally. Choose **Connect Wallet** and sign the authentication message. This signature proves wallet ownership; it does not authorize payment.

## 2. Select Arc Testnet

- Chain ID: `5042002` (`0x4cef52`)
- RPC: `https://rpc.testnet.arc.network`
- Native asset: test USDC

Obtain test assets through the official Arc/Circle testnet resources. Never send Mainnet assets to this MVP. Keep a gas reserve.

## 3. Create a deposit intent

Open Funds, choose Top up USDC and enter an exact amount with up to six decimal places. If the receiver is not configured, funding is unavailable. Do not invent a destination address.

## 4. Transfer and verify

The wallet signs a direct test-USDC transfer to the address stored on the intent. Alternatively transfer manually. Paste the transaction hash and select Verify & credit after two confirmations. Submission alone never creates balance.

## 5. Place a test order

Choose a test product in Shop. Confirm the quantity and your reference. The backend reserves and captures exact test USDC atomically and records simulated fulfillment. Inspect Orders and Transactions.

See [funding safety](/docs/account/funds) before transferring. Withdrawals are not implemented in this MVP.
